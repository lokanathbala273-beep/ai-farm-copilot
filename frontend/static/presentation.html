The Agricultural Dilemma

The Core Problem: Indian Agriculture's Three Disconnected Silos

A farmer's journey is continuous, but existing AgriTech tools force them to piece together 3 isolated apps with zero shared data.

❌ Silo 1: Advisory Gap

No Personalised Multimodal Guidance

  • • Generic & Text-Heavy: Boilerplate advice ignoring local dialect (Odia, Sambalpuri, Hindi).
  • • Ignores Visuals: Farmers cannot show leaf damage to get verified medical chemical dosages.
  • • Memoryless: Forgets historical soil chemistry (NPK, pH), prior sprays, and crop stages.
❌ Silo 2: Risk Gap

Late & Unexplained Black-Box Alerts

  • • Reactive, Not Proactive: Threats are flagged after crops have already rotted or withered.
  • • Opaque Warnings: Tells farmers 'High Risk' without explaining WHY (moisture + temp + pathogen).
  • • Lacks Actionability: Alarms cause panic without providing concrete, scientifically verified IPM steps.
❌ Silo 3: Market Gap

Blind "What & Where to Sell" Decisions

  • • Sowing Blindness: Planting without understanding market demand or impending harvest gluts.
  • • The Mandi Illusion: High gross price at distant mandis wiped out by transport and 1.5% APMC fees.
  • • Distress Selling: Farmers sell at rock-bottom prices unaware of cold-storage viability and price trends.

Module 2 Deep Dive

Crop Risk Intelligence: Explainable Multi-Threat Early Warning

🔍 Explainable AI (XAI) Principle: 'No Opaque Red Alerts.' Every warning details: Observed Condition → Biological Cause → Concrete Mitigation.
🦠 Outbreak Risk

Disease Spore Invasions

• Triggers: Humidity > 85%, Canopy 24-29°C.
• Why: Damp canopy creates fungal spore germination.
• Mitigation: Preventative copper oxychloride spray.

🐛 Pest Pressure

Infestation Waves

• Triggers: Degree-day thermal spikes.
• Why: Temperature window accelerates Stem Borer hatchings.
• Mitigation: Deploy pheromone traps & neem oil.

🌦️ Microclimate

Weather Extremes

• Triggers: Live GPS satellite sync.
• Why: 42mm downpour in 6h causes root suffocation.
• Mitigation: Open drainage furrows immediately.

📉 Market Crash

Price Volatility

• Triggers: District oversupply arrivals.
• Why: Cuttack arrivals up 40%, price crash in 48h.
• Mitigation: Hold in cold room or reroute to BBSR.
